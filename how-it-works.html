Journal3 combines a beautiful, mindful journaling experience with the immutable permanence of Web3.

The Philosophy

We believe that personal reflections, daily moods, and meaningful memories deserve to be preserved securely. Traditional journaling apps store your most private thoughts on centralized servers, vulnerable to being lost, sold, or locked behind paywalls.

Journal3 changes this by putting you in total control. By leveraging the BOT Chain ledger, your journal entries are permanently etched into the blockchain, bound only to your cryptographic wallet.

1. Writing On-Chain

When you log a mood (from a 1-5 scale) and write your thoughts, Journal3 does not send this data to a traditional database. Instead, it interacts directly with our custom smart contract via ethers.js.

The text is stored on-chain inside a struct. Because blockchains are immutable, we designed a "soft delete" system. If you choose to delete an entry, the app hides it from your view, but the blockchain maintains the historical record of your thoughts forever.

2. Decentralized Photos via IPFS

Storing binary image data on a blockchain is prohibitively expensive and inefficient. To solve this, Journal3 utilizes the InterPlanetary File System (IPFS).

When you attach a photo to your entry, you can configure your own Pinata API key in the app settings. The image is pinned securely to IPFS, and only the lightweight CID (Content Identifier) string is saved on-chain, keeping gas costs incredibly low while maintaining true decentralization.

3. Minting Memories as NFTs

Some days are more special than others. For those moments, Journal3 allows you to mint a specific journal entry as a 1-of-1 ERC-721 NFT.

This process takes the metadata of your entry—the mood, the date, a text excerpt, and the attached photo—and immutably binds it to an NFT on the BOT Chain. It's a digital monument to a specific day in your life, held securely in your own wallet.
